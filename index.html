Our Menu

Hot Coffee

  • Americano - Espresso mixed with hot water for a smooth and balanced taste.
  • Latte - Espresso with steamed milk and a light layer of foam.
  • Cappuccino - Espresso with steamed milk and rich milk foam.

Cold Drinks

  • Iced Americano - Refreshing espresso served with cold water and ice.
  • Iced Latte - Smooth espresso with cold milk and ice.
  • Mocha - A delicious combination of coffee, milk and chocolate.
